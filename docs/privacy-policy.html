Thyroid Echo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Thyroid Echo is designed as a private, local-first thyroid tracking journal. It helps users record information they choose to enter and organize it for their own use and for discussions with healthcare professionals.

Our privacy promise: The developers of Thyroid Echo do not receive, collect, or store the personal or health information you enter into your journal, and they do not have remote access to it. No account or internet connection is required for core tracking.

Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, Thyroid Echo may store locally on your device an optional first name, thyroid condition, medication information and history, medication status logs, symptom and wellbeing check-ins, context tags, weight, laboratory values and reference ranges, notes, reminder preferences, and accessibility or optional-feature settings. This information is not available to the Thyroid Echo developers.

Offline use

Daily check-ins, medication reminders, journal history, labs, trends, doctor reports, settings, and local encrypted backup and restore are designed to work without an internet connection. The current app does not request Android's Internet permission.

Encrypted backup and restore

Thyroid Echo can create a password-protected backup file. The backup is encrypted on your device before it is written to a location you choose. Thyroid Echo does not store the backup password and cannot recover it for you. The app does not automatically upload backups to a Thyroid Echo server.

Android backup

Android backup rules intentionally exclude Thyroid Echo journal data from normal Android cloud backup and restore. Users who want portability can create their own encrypted Thyroid Echo backup.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Thyroid Echo does not include third-party advertising or analytics. The app does not sell personal or health information, and there is no developer dashboard containing users' journals, symptoms, medication logs, lab results, or notes.

Notifications

If you enable medication reminders, Thyroid Echo uses Android notification and alarm features on your device. Reminder text intentionally avoids displaying medication name or dose.

Reports and sharing

Thyroid Echo can create patient-entered text summaries and PDF reports on your device. A report leaves the app only when you explicitly choose to share it through Android. After you share information to another app or service, that destination's privacy practices apply.

Permissions

The app may request permissions needed for optional medication reminders, including notification and exact-alarm functionality, and it listens for device reboot or time changes so enabled reminders can be restored. These permissions are not used to collect health information from other apps or services.

Data deletion

Because journal data is stored locally, uninstalling Thyroid Echo removes the app's local journal data from that device. Before uninstalling or moving phones, users can create an encrypted backup and restore it on another compatible installation.

Medical information and safety

Thyroid Echo is a patient-entered tracking and organization tool. It does not diagnose thyroid disease, interpret whether laboratory values are normal or abnormal, determine whether a medication or dose is medically appropriate, recommend medication changes, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. The disclaimer is shown during first-run setup and remains available later in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If Thyroid Echo later adds functionality that changes how information is transmitted or shared, this policy will be updated before release. The core goal remains that users can maintain a useful thyroid journal without giving the developers access to their personal health information.

Contact

A public Thyroid Echo support and privacy contact address will be added before the closed beta or public Play listing is submitted for review.
