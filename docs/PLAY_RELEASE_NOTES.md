# Play release notes

## v0.3.4 — Internal test candidate

Thyroid Echo is now prepared for its first Google Play internal test.

- faster symptom check-in: answer Yes/No before opening symptom details
- optional local first-name greeting and larger-text mode
- medication reminders with follow-up when no medication log is saved
- Today-at-a-glance status card
- optional context tags and weight tracking
- improved History with top reported symptoms and severity
- standard thyroid labs with proper calendar date selection
- optional expanded thyroid antibody fields: TPOAb, TgAb, and TRAb
- medication/dose-change history
- doctor-ready text and PDF summaries
- local-first journal with no account, ads, or analytics

Thyroid Echo records patient-entered information and does not diagnose, interpret lab results, or recommend medication changes.
