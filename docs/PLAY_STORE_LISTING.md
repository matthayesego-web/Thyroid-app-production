# Thyroid Echo — Google Play listing draft

## App title

**Thyroid Echo: Symptom Tracker**

## Short description

**Track thyroid symptoms, medication and labs. Create doctor-ready summaries.**

## Full description

Thyroid Echo is a simple, private thyroid journal built to help you keep the information you want for everyday tracking and medical appointments in one place.

Track hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism without turning your day into a long questionnaire. A daily check-in can be as simple as logging medication, answering whether you have symptoms today, and saving. If you do have symptoms, Thyroid Echo opens the relevant symptom list so you can record their intensity.

### Track what matters

- daily thyroid symptoms and severity
- medication status: Taken, Late, or Missed
- overall wellbeing, energy, mood, and sleep
- optional context such as poor sleep, stress, illness, exercise, travel, or diet changes
- optional weight tracking
- TSH, Free T4, T3, and laboratory reference ranges
- optional expanded thyroid antibody tracking for TPOAb, TgAb, and TRAb
- clinician-directed medication and dose-change milestones

### Prepare for appointments

Create a patient-entered summary that brings together recent check-ins, symptom patterns, medication history, and lab results. Share the summary as text or generate a PDF to bring to an appointment.

### Simple by default

Optional features can be switched on only when you need them. Thyroid Echo is designed to stay calm and quick for everyday use rather than filling the screen with every possible health field.

### Private by design

Your journal is stored locally on your device. Thyroid Echo does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics. Health information is not uploaded to a Thyroid Echo server during normal use.

### Free for everyone

Thyroid Echo is intended to remain free to use. Core health tracking, history, reports, reminders, and portability features are not intended to be locked behind a subscription.

### Important

Thyroid Echo is a patient-entered tracking and organization tool. It does not diagnose thyroid disease, interpret laboratory results, determine whether medication is effective or medically appropriate, recommend medication doses, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
