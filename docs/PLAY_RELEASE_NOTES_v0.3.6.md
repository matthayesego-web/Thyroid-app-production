# Thyroid Echo v0.3.6 — Internal Test

## Release name

`Thyroid Echo v0.3.6 – Privacy & Polish`

## Google Play release notes

- Simplified medical disclaimers so they appear during first-run setup and remain available in Settings instead of repeating across everyday screens.
- Strengthened privacy messaging: journal information stays on the device and is not accessible to the developers.
- Clarified that core tracking, reminders, labs, reports, and encrypted backup/restore work without an internet connection.
- Continued UI/navigation polish from v0.3.5.

## Internal test focus

When updating from v0.3.4 or v0.3.5 through Google Play, verify that existing journal entries, medication settings, labs, and preferences remain intact. Do not uninstall before testing update persistence.
