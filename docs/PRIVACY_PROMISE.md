# Thyroid Echo privacy promise

Thyroid Echo is designed so the developers do not receive, collect, or store the personal or health information you enter into the app.

- Journal entries, symptoms, medication logs, lab values, notes, preferences, and your optional first name are stored locally on your device.
- The developers have no remote access to the information stored in your Thyroid Echo journal.
- Thyroid Echo does not require an account, advertising identifier, analytics profile, or remote health-data database.
- Core app functions — daily check-ins, medication reminders, journal history, labs, trends, doctor reports, and local encrypted backup/restore — remain usable without an internet connection.
- Reports or encrypted backup files leave the app only when the user explicitly chooses to export, save, or share them through Android.
- Future optional online features must remain opt-in and must not change the offline availability of the core journal.

Medical disclaimer: Thyroid Echo records and summarizes user-entered information. It does not diagnose thyroid disease, interpret whether a medication dose is medically correct, or recommend treatment changes.
